Current features and features in development

This policy covers the MemoriVerse website, support and the app features available in your build. MemoriVerse is being prepared for general release. Linked family accounts, independent-teen transitions and KWS production verification are not publicly activated.

Who is responsible

MemoriVerse is operated by Yolanda King.

For privacy questions or requests, email memoriverseapp@gmail.com. Please do not send passwords, parent or child PINs, payment-card details, identity documents, or a child's private learning history unless a safe, necessary process has first been agreed with you.

The ways you can use MemoriVerse

Guest learning saves learning and preferences on the device without requiring an account. Original Child Mode, intended for parent-supervised ages 6–12, uses its own local save. It uses typed recall, does not use the microphone for recitation, and does not enable account/cloud or social access in that mode. Its parent PIN protects mode switching; it is not online identity verification.

Ordinary accounts provide sign-in and cloud saving and synchronisation through Supabase. The ordinary-account signup screen uses a 13+ age declaration. This product threshold is not a statement that every 13-year-old can consent independently in every country. MemoriVerse is being prepared for general release; availability and consent requirements depend on the applicable country and account type.

Linked family accounts, their child social features, independent-teen transitions and KWS parent verification are in gated development and are not available as a live family service. KWS Parent Verification is accessible in our Test environment; no operational app-to-provider verification flow or real country approval is claimed. We will provide the required direct parent/child notices and choices before introducing such processing. Verifying an adult is not, by itself, proof of guardianship or consent for a child.

Family Accounts and Parental Controls

Where linked family accounts are enabled, parents manage their children's accounts, privacy settings, friendships and leaderboard participation. Parents can also view their children's Scripture-learning progress.

MemoriVerse uses parental verification and consent procedures where required. Children's social features are private by default and require appropriate parental permissions.

Children under 13 do not receive advertising. Children can earn and spend Seeds in the Seed Shop without requiring parental approval for individual purchases.

Scripture learning and sensitive information

Scripture memorisation records what you practise and your learning progress. It is not a declaration of your religion or beliefs. MemoriVerse does not infer, classify or profile religious beliefs from learning activity, and does not need a religion or denomination label for you to learn.

A username or public ranking can still disclose participation in MemoriVerse. Pseudonymous information is not anonymous when it can be linked to a person. Please avoid adding unnecessary private information to answers or support messages.

Optional speech and audio

Outside original Child Mode, you can choose supported device speech recognition instead of typing. It needs the relevant microphone/speech permission. Depending on the device, operating system, language and settings, the speech provider may process audio off the device under its own terms. We do not promise that speech recognition is entirely offline.

The app requests recognition without persisting an audio recording file. However, recognised text, your corrected text and the answer method can be saved as part of the lesson and included in the synced account save. You can use typed recall instead and manage microphone/speech permissions in device settings. Narration is spoken output, separate from microphone recognition; device voice services have their own processing behaviour.

Friends, rankings and advertising

Joining ordinary-account Friends is an explicit choice. Exact-username discovery, friends rankings and public rankings all start off. You can change each choice in the app's social privacy settings. Each ranking choice controls whether you are listed; the public ranking is viewed by signed-in Friends members in the app. Rankings show usernames, lifetime Seeds and positions, with your own row marked.

Friend requests use a privately shared friend code. Friends and requests show usernames and connection/request status. Discovery controls search visibility; turning it off does not end existing friendships or cancel code-based actions with already-known people. Do not put a real name, school, email address or contact information in a child's username. These ordinary-account choices are separate from the disabled linked-family permission system.

The current app contains no advertising or analytics SDKs. Children under 13 are ad-free, and no advertising identifiers or behavioural ad tracking are enabled for the family design. The disabled linked-family design keeps child social features off until the applicable parent permissions and country safeguards are satisfied. Children may spend earned Seeds without a guardian approving each purchase. This does not mean that network providers keep no security logs.

Providers, hosting and the website

Supabase provides account authentication and cloud database services. The confirmed primary project region is West EU (Ireland), eu-west-1. That is the primary project-data location, not a promise that every provider operation, support activity or subprocessor stays in Ireland.

The MemoriVerse website is a static GitHub Pages site. It contains no contact form, analytics or tracking cookies set by us. GitHub logs visitor IP addresses for security when serving Pages. Website hosting therefore is not “no data collection”. See GitHub Pages data collection.

Support email is handled through Gmail. Google receives the message and related email information when you contact that address. See Google's privacy policy.

Device speech services, app stores and hosting providers may process information under their own policies.

Providers may process information outside your country. Supabase describes its processing locations and contractual transfer safeguards in its data residency and transfers information. GitHub, Google and device-service providers describe their own processing in their privacy notices. You can contact us for information about the arrangements applicable to your request. A primary project region is not a promise of EU-only processing.

If KWS Parent Verification is later enabled, its separate notice will explain the account email and verification context sent to KWS, and the proof retained by MemoriVerse. The planned family integration is designed to retain a limited adult-verification receipt and consent evidence; it does not request or store verification identity documents, card details, photographs or face-scan material. Hashes linked to an account remain personal information. KWS adult verification does not establish guardian authority or give consent for a child.

KWS separately operates AgeGraph, its reusable verification network. Its retention and opt-out process are separate from deleting a MemoriVerse account or withdrawing MemoriVerse consent. See the KWS privacy policy and verification FAQ. No statement here means KWS already receives MemoriVerse parent or child data in a live family flow.

Why information is processed

We use learning, account and reward information to provide the learning and cloud-save services you request, preserve progress and ownership, operate optional social features, maintain security and respond to support or privacy requests. Account sign-in and cloud synchronisation need account and save information; guest learning and original Child Mode do not require an online account.

Where UK or European data-protection law applies, our current website operation, necessary security and routine support use our legitimate interests in providing a reliable service, preventing misuse and responding to people who contact us. These interests must be balanced against your rights, with particular care for children. We use information to comply with legal obligations where a specific duty applies, including handling applicable privacy-rights requests.

For account and learning services, the applicable basis must support the service relationship and the particular country and age group. The purpose-specific legal and consent arrangements for wider account availability and new family services are still being finalised before general release. This policy does not establish worldwide eligibility or substitute for a required consent process. Optional permissions are separate from account access; where we rely on consent, you can withdraw it without changing the lawfulness of earlier processing.

MemoriVerse does not infer religious beliefs, create religious profiles, or make decisions about eligibility for employment, credit or similar significant matters from your learning. If information you choose to send itself reveals sensitive personal matters, it may need additional protection; please avoid sending unnecessary sensitive details.

Retention, deletion and recovery

We preserve active users’ Scripture progress, mastery, Seeds, Seed Shop purchases, trophies and room arrangements for their continuing learning and ownership. Inactivity alone must never trigger account or learning-data deletion, including after human review. A valid erasure request or a separately justified, lawful closure is different from inactivity.

Local guest learning, original Child Mode and individual account saves are separate. Removing an account is not a request to delete another local profile or another child’s learning. Sign-out, a parent PIN change or switching profiles is not a request to erase learning. Do not uninstall just to fix a sign-in problem: an unsynchronised device-only save may be lost.

Approved retention targets

The following are our approved directions, not a claim that automatic cleanup is already operating. Retention enforcement and some account-management procedures are still being completed. These periods apply only to information we control, where justified and technically enforceable; they are not promises about suppliers’ internal copies.

  • Necessary security records: a target of up to 90 days from the event, with earlier deletion when no longer needed. A specific incident or legal duty may justify a restricted, documented hold with a review or end date.
  • Resolved support correspondence: a target of up to 12 months after resolution where justified, with unnecessary messages or attachments removed sooner. The support-mail deletion workflow is not yet fully implemented.
  • Parental-consent evidence for future family services: retain only the minimum proof while the consent remains relevant and legally necessary. An additional period of up to 12 months after its purpose ends is provisionally accepted, subject to applicable law and a documented continuing need. Withdrawal requires reassessment; it does not automatically start another 12-month term. This exception does not justify retaining a full learning history or identity documents.

There is no routine inactivity deletion. A future child-cloud closure would require human review, a documented lawful reason independent of inactivity or unanswered notices, and appropriate learning-data preservation, renewal or export options. Notices are planned 60 and 30 days before closure where feasible, with failures reviewed. That procedure is not yet operational. Notice arrangements cannot override valid erasure rights or extend processing without a lawful basis.

How to request account or data deletion

For an ordinary account in a build with account management, open Profile → Account → Delete my account. You must be online and re-enter your password before confirming permanent deletion. The final confirmation may refer to your account and cloud save as a “backup” in earlier builds. Check that you are signed into the correct account before continuing.

The existing server deletion operation removes that ordinary account’s current cloud profile and learning save, personal reward and purchase evidence, and related personal social and ranking records. Shared Scripture catalogues remain. Deletion is permanent and affects ownership and learning in that account. If you need a copy first, contact us before confirming; a universal self-service export is not yet available in released builds.

If you cannot sign in, the deletion option is unavailable, or you need to delete only particular information, email memoriverseapp@gmail.com with “MemoriVerse data deletion” in the subject. Identify the account or local profile concerned and the scope of the request. We may need a proportionate identity or guardian-authority check before acting. Do not send passwords, PINs, identity documents or a child’s learning history in the first message.

Local cleanup and interrupted-deletion recovery are being improved. A cloud deletion does not prove that every device copy has been erased. Some local setup/recovery information may remain, and the app cannot remotely wipe other offline devices, separate guest or original Child Mode saves, exported files or all physical database remnants. If a deletion result is uncertain or cleanup fails, contact support before uninstalling or clearing the device. No complete device-erasure or recovery guarantee is made.

Future linked-family erasure must identify the correct learner, verify the requester and respect independent teenagers’ rights. A parent deleting their login must not silently delete a child’s permanent identity, learning, Seeds, purchases or room. The deliberate family-erasure and preservation/export procedures remain in development and are not a publicly available service. Consent expiry, withdrawal, PIN reset or a failed sign-in must not be treated as instructions to erase learning.

Provider logs, support mail and infrastructure copies require separate handling. Published log-access windows do not prove deletion of all provider copies. We do not promise a recoverable backup, a fixed provider-backup expiry or instant erasure everywhere, and no separate Supabase backup is required.

Recovery and consent choices

Ordinary account password recovery is available through Sign In → Forgot Password?, using your account email and the recovery link. Original offline Child Mode uses a local parent PIN; a lost PIN or local key is not automatically recoverable. Contact support before removing an installation containing learning.

You can turn off optional social visibility and ranking choices in the app’s social privacy settings. Turning discovery off does not itself end friendships; remove or block a connection separately if needed. For consent-related questions or withdrawal requests, use the privacy contact below. Future guardian verification, consent withdrawal and recovery services will need their own notices and safeguards before activation.

Your choices and rights

Depending on your location and circumstances, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or obtain a copy of personal information, restrict or object to certain processing, and withdraw consent where consent is used. Withdrawing consent does not retrospectively change the lawfulness of earlier processing. Some rights have legal exceptions and may need a proportionate identity check.

Contact memoriverseapp@gmail.com to make a request or privacy complaint. Tell us which account or profile and which information your request concerns. We will assess the request under the applicable law, explain any identity check or limitation, and respond within the applicable legal time limit. You can also complain to your relevant data-protection regulator. For the UK, this is the Information Commissioner's Office.

We will update this notice when the service or its processing changes and identify the effective date. Material new family, social or verification processing will need its own review, notices and permissions; updating this page alone does not authorise it.
