MemoriVerse learning and Family services

This policy describes how MemoriVerse handles information on this website and in the current app, including its learning, account and Family features. Access to particular account features depends on your country, age and the required permissions.

The ways you can use MemoriVerse

Guest learning saves learning and preferences on the device without requiring an account. Original Child Mode, intended for parent-supervised ages 6–12, uses its own local save. It uses typed recall, does not use the microphone for recitation, and does not enable account/cloud or social access in that mode. Its parent PIN protects mode switching; it is not online identity verification.

Ordinary email accounts provide sign-in, cloud saving and synchronisation through Supabase. The signup age check includes a 13+ declaration. Turning 13 does not automatically establish independent consent or account eligibility in every country; the applicable age and consent requirements still apply.

Linked family accounts give each child a permanent learner identity, a child username and PIN, authorised-device access and a parent-managed account. Linking an existing Child Mode profile is optional. The original local profile stays separate; a transfer must verify the complete learning and ownership records before the linked profile is activated.

Independent teenage accounts use the same learner identity when the applicable age and country requirements are met. An eligible teenager can establish a verified email/password login without losing learning, Seeds, purchases, trophies, room arrangements or friendships. Parent dashboard access ends unless the teenager explicitly permits continued sharing. Social permissions remain restrictive and do not switch on automatically.

Family Accounts and Parental Controls

Parents manage linked child profiles, privacy settings, friendships and leaderboard participation. The Parent Dashboard shows Scripture learning, mastery, review progress, streaks, Seeds, trophies, purchases and learning paths.

MemoriVerse uses adult verification, guardian-authority checks and consent procedures where required. Verifying adulthood is not, by itself, proof of guardianship or consent for a child. Friendships, the Friends Leaderboard and the Public Leaderboard start off for children. A verified, authorised parent controls each permission separately. Enabling the Public Leaderboard records a new, explicit authorisation for that child’s participation; earlier consent records are not rewritten. Turning it off removes the child from public results without changing learning, Seeds, friendships or Friends Leaderboard participation.

When Friends is enabled for both accounts, a managed child connects automatically to the verified parent who created their account, using their existing family relationship. This connection does not need a friend code or a separate request and does not connect the child to other adults. Requests between children require approval from both guardians. Blocking, consent withdrawal and loss of the required parent authority restrict the connection. Country and age requirements continue to apply.

Children under 13 do not receive advertising. Children can earn and spend Seeds without parental approval for individual purchases. Existing ownership, original receipts and costs, balances, equipped items and room arrangements are preserved when a profile is linked. Owned items remain available offline; new purchases by linked child accounts require an internet connection.

Parent notice versions for new Family admissions

These notices are published for the prepared launch configuration. Publication does not itself enable new countries or replace notices already accepted by existing families. The app shows the notice applicable to a supported new admission in its existing notice area, with the same purpose choices.

Scripture learning and sensitive information

Selected Scriptures, answers and progress can reveal participation in religious learning, even though they are not a declaration of your religion or beliefs. MemoriVerse does not infer, classify or profile religious beliefs from learning activity, and does not need a religion or denomination label for you to learn.

A username or public ranking can still disclose participation in MemoriVerse. Pseudonymous information is not anonymous when it can be linked to a person. Please avoid adding unnecessary private information to answers or support messages.

Optional speech and audio

Outside original Child Mode, you can choose supported device speech recognition instead of typing. It needs the relevant microphone/speech permission. Depending on the device, operating system, language and settings, the speech provider may process audio off the device under its own terms. We do not promise that speech recognition is entirely offline.

The app requests recognition without persisting an audio recording file. However, recognised text, your corrected text and the answer method can be saved as part of the lesson and included in the synced account save. You can use typed recall instead and manage microphone/speech permissions in device settings. Narration is spoken output, separate from microphone recognition; device voice services have their own processing behaviour.

Friends, rankings and advertising

Joining ordinary-account Friends is an explicit choice. Exact-username discovery, friends rankings and public rankings all start off. You can change each choice in the app's social privacy settings. Each ranking choice controls whether you are listed; the public ranking can be viewed by signed-in app users without joining Friends or choosing to appear in the ranking. Rankings show usernames, lifetime Seeds and positions, with your own row marked. Public entries do not include display names, account emails, age, location, photographs, family relationships or private learning histories.

Friend requests use a privately shared friend code; the creating-parent connection described above is an exception to that request process. Friends and requests show usernames and connection/request status. Discovery controls search visibility; turning it off does not end existing friendships or cancel code-based actions with already-known people. Do not put a real name, school, email address or contact information in a child's username. Children’s social access also requires the separate parent permissions described above.

MemoriVerse does not show advertising in this release. The updated iOS app connects ordinary signed-in accounts to RevenueCat’s purchase-service infrastructure while purchases and paid upgrades remain switched off. Ad attribution, automatic advertising-identifier collection and SDK diagnostics are disabled. Children under 13 remain ad-free, with no behavioural ad tracking. Child social features remain off until the applicable parent permissions and country safeguards are satisfied. Network and authentication providers may still keep necessary security records.

Why information is processed

We use learning, account and reward information to provide the learning and cloud-save services you request, preserve progress and ownership, operate optional social features, maintain security and respond to support or privacy requests. Account sign-in and cloud synchronisation need account and save information; guest learning and original Child Mode do not require an online account.

Where UK or European data-protection law applies, website operation, necessary security and routine support use our legitimate interests in providing a reliable service, preventing misuse and responding to people who contact us. These interests are balanced against your rights, with particular care for children. We use information to comply with legal obligations where a specific duty applies, including applicable privacy-rights requests.

Account and learning processing needs a lawful basis appropriate to the service, country and age group. Before making an account service available, we establish that basis and explain any required consent during account setup. Optional social permissions are separate from account access. Where we rely on consent, you can withdraw it without changing the lawfulness of earlier processing. Country eligibility is checked separately; this policy is not a statement of worldwide eligibility.

MemoriVerse does not infer religious beliefs, create religious profiles, or make decisions about eligibility for employment, credit or similar significant matters from your learning. If information you choose to send itself reveals sensitive personal matters, it may need additional protection; please avoid sending unnecessary sensitive details.

Retention, deletion and recovery

We preserve active users’ Scripture progress, mastery, Seeds, Seed Shop purchases, trophies and room arrangements for their continuing learning and ownership. Inactivity alone must never trigger account or learning-data deletion, including after human review. A valid erasure request or a separately justified, lawful closure is different from inactivity.

Local guest learning, original Child Mode and individual account saves are separate. Removing an account is not a request to delete another local profile or another child’s learning. Sign-out, a parent PIN change or switching profiles is not a request to erase learning. Do not uninstall just to fix a sign-in problem: an unsynchronised device-only save may be lost.

Retention rules and targets

We keep personal information only while needed for its stated purpose or a justified legal requirement. The targets below apply to records we control, where justified and technically enforceable. They do not set retention periods for suppliers’ internal records or promise automatic removal from every system at the same moment.

  • Necessary security records: a target of up to 90 days from the event, with earlier deletion when no longer needed. A specific incident or legal duty may justify a restricted, documented hold with a review or end date.
  • Resolved support correspondence: a target of up to 12 months after resolution where justified, with unnecessary messages or attachments removed sooner.
  • Parental-consent evidence: retain only the minimum proof while consent remains relevant and legally necessary. After that purpose ends, the target is no more than 12 additional months where there is a documented continuing need, subject to applicable law. Withdrawal requires reassessment; it does not automatically start another 12-month term. Any legally necessary exception needs a specific reason and review. This does not justify retaining a full learning history or identity documents.

There is no routine inactivity deletion. Any proposed child-cloud closure requires human review, a documented lawful reason independent of inactivity or unanswered notices, and appropriate learning-data preservation, renewal or export options. We plan notices 60 and 30 days before closure where feasible, with notice failures reviewed. Notice arrangements do not override valid erasure rights or extend processing without a lawful basis.

How to request account or data deletion

For an ordinary email account, open Profile → Account → Delete my account. You must be online and re-enter your password before confirming permanent deletion. Check that you are signed into the correct account before continuing.

Deleting an ordinary email account removes that account’s current cloud profile and learning save, personal reward and purchase evidence, and related personal social and ranking records. Shared Scripture catalogues remain. Deletion is permanent and affects ownership and learning in that account; a parent’s login and a child’s permanent learner identity are handled separately.

Before deletion, you can use Export my device learning in the account panel to obtain that account’s saved learning, rewards, purchases and room records from the current device, including progress waiting to sync. Check that the exported file has been saved to your chosen destination and keep it private. Family or cloud-data exports can be requested through the applicable privacy controls or our privacy contact. An export does not provide an automatic restoration service, and you are not required to purchase or create a separate backup.

If you cannot sign in, the deletion option is unavailable, or you need to delete only particular information, email memoriverseapp@gmail.com with “MemoriVerse data deletion” in the subject. Identify the account or local profile concerned and the scope of the request. We may need a proportionate identity or guardian-authority check before acting. Do not send passwords, PINs, identity documents or a child’s learning history in the first message.

Confirmed deletion includes cleanup of the selected account’s local records. An uncertain server result does not authorise erasing its local learning. The app may keep that save locked while the outcome is resolved. If deletion is interrupted or cleanup fails, contact support before uninstalling or clearing the device.

Cloud deletion does not remotely wipe other offline devices, separate guest or original Child Mode saves, exported files or all physical storage remnants. Each device copy needs appropriate handling. We do not promise complete forensic erasure or guaranteed recovery.

For linked child and independent learner accounts, use the learner privacy controls or contact us. Deleting a learner requires the correct learner to be identified, the requester’s authority to be verified and independent teenagers’ rights to be respected. The process offers preservation/export choices and separate, deliberate confirmation. An unresolved account transition or changes to the saved information require review before erasure can finish. A parent deleting their login does not silently delete a child’s permanent identity, learning, Seeds, purchases or room. Consent expiry, withdrawal, PIN reset and failed sign-in are not instructions to erase learning.

Provider logs, support mail and infrastructure copies require separate handling. Published log-access windows do not prove deletion of all provider copies. We do not promise a recoverable backup, a fixed provider-backup expiry or instant erasure everywhere, and no separate Supabase backup is required.
